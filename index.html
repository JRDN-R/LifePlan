2. Daily Nutrition Targets

Calories: 1,900-2,200/dayProtein: 180-220g/dayWater: 100-130 oz/dayDaily weigh-in

The fat loss comes from the calorie deficit. Training protects the muscle. Protein supports recovery and helps keep the cut from turning into just being smaller and softer.

Food rules

  • Build every meal around protein first.
  • Use clean carbs like rice, potatoes, oatmeal, fruit, beans, and vegetables.
  • Keep fats controlled because they are calorie-dense.
  • Measure creamer. Do not free-pour it.
  • Water only besides coffee.
  • No soda, juice, random snacking, sweets, late-night calorie creep, or casual bites throughout the day.

Simple daily meal structure

Meal 1Greek yogurt + protein powder + fruit, or eggs/egg whites + oatmeal.
Meal 2Chicken, turkey, lean beef, or fish + rice/potatoes + vegetables.
Meal 3Protein shake or Greek yogurt.
Meal 4Lean protein + vegetables + small carb serving if calories allow.

3. Training Method: Make Light Work Heavy

The goal is not just to move. The goal is to create enough mechanical tension, metabolic stress, and controlled fatigue to preserve or rebuild muscle while body fat drops.

ConcentricLift with intent. Move fast but clean. Think explosive without getting sloppy.
EccentricLower slowly for 3-5 seconds. Own the negative. This is where light weight becomes useful.
IsometricPause in the hardest position or squeeze the target muscle for 1-3 seconds.
Main tempo prescriptionUse 1-3-1 on most work: 1 second concentric, 3 second eccentric, 1 second isometric pause. On easier movements, use 1-5-2 to make the set harder.
Intensity targetMost working sets should finish with 1-3 reps in reserve. Push-ups, rows, squats, lunges, and circuits can go closer to failure as long as form stays clean.
Do not just chase sorenessThe point is progression. Add reps, add rounds, shorten rest, slow the eccentric, increase the isometric pause, improve range of motion, or make the exercise harder.

4. Weekly Workout Plan

Monday, Push + Core + 1 Mile

Tempo focus: controlled eccentric pressing. Use 1-3-1 on push-ups and dips.

  • Push-ups5 sets near failure
  • Pike push-ups4 sets, 8-15
  • Chair dips3 sets, 10-20
  • Plank3 sets, 45-60 sec
  • Leg raises3 sets, 12-20
  • Crunches3 sets, 20-30
  • Walk/jog1 mile
Tuesday, Legs + HIIT

Tempo focus: slow eccentrics and bottom-position isometrics. Use 1-4-1 on squats and lunges.

  • Bodyweight squats5 sets, 20-30
  • Reverse lunges4 sets, 10-15/leg
  • Glute bridges4 sets, 20-30
  • Calf raises4 sets, 25-40

HIIT circuit

Repeat 5-8 rounds. Move hard during the work, then actually recover during the rest.

  • Mountain climbers30 sec
  • Running in place30 sec
  • Burpees or squat thrusts30 sec
  • Rest60-90 sec
Wednesday, Pull + Core + Walking

Tempo focus: squeeze the back hard. Rows should use a 1-2 second isometric hold at the top and a 3-4 second eccentric.

  • Dumbbell rows with 20s5 sets, 15-25/arm
  • Rear delt raises4 sets, 15-25
  • Curls4 sets, 12-20
  • Farmer carries4 rounds
  • Side plank3 sets/side
  • Dead bugs3 sets/side
  • Easy walk30-45 min
Thursday, Cardio Day

Walk/jog intervals for 1.5-2 miles.

Starting version2 min fast walk, 1 min jog, repeat until done.
Progression version1 min walk, 2 min jog, repeat until done.
EventuallyStraight jogging.
Friday, Full-Body Tension Circuit

Tempo focus: every rep is clean. No sloppy speed reps. Use 1-3-1 on strength movements, normal speed on mountain climbers.

Do 5 rounds:

  • Push-ups10-20
  • Dumbbell rows15-20/arm
  • Bodyweight squats25-30
  • Reverse lunges10/leg
  • Mountain climbers30 sec
  • Plank45 sec
  • Rest between rounds1-2 min
Saturday, Conditioning + Pump Work

Tempo focus: conditioning first, then pump/isometric work.

  • Walk/jog2 miles
  • Burpees or squat thrusts5 sets of 10
  • Push-up bottom holds3 x 15-30 sec
  • Wall sit or squat hold3 x 30-60 sec
  • Crunches3 x 25
  • Leg raises3 x 12-20
Sunday, Rest or Easy Walk

Rest or easy walk. Do not turn the rest day into another full workout.

5. 20 lb Dumbbell Rules

Twenty-pound dumbbells are light for some movements, so make them hard through tempo, range of motion, unilateral work, and pauses.

Main dumbbell tempo1-4-2 when the weight feels too easy: drive up with intent, lower for 4 seconds, hold/squeeze for 2 seconds.

Best dumbbell exercises

  • Dumbbell rows with top squeeze
  • Goblet squats with bottom pause
  • Romanian deadlifts with slow eccentric hamstring stretch
  • Floor press with pause near the bottom
  • Shoulder press with controlled negative
  • Curls with hard peak contraction
  • Farmer carries for grip, traps, core, and conditioning

Weeks 3-4Add volumeAdd reps or one extra round. Keep eccentrics controlled.
Weeks 5-8Increase densityShorten rest, increase cardio distance, and push circuits harder.

Progression hierarchyFirst improve form, then reps, then rounds, then shorter rest, then harder variation, then slower eccentric or longer isometric hold.
